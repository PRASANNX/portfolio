## 5. INDIAN AUDIENCE TUNING GUIDE

### 5.1 Language Adjustments for Indian Audience

| Western Copy | Indian-Optimized Copy | Reason |
|-------------|----------------------|--------|
| "Ship fast" | "Launch in 48 hours" | Specificity over abstraction |
| "Get started for free" | "Start at ₹4,999" | Indian buyers want transparent pricing |
| "Join our community" | "Join 1,000+ Indian founders" | Social proof with local context |
| "Built with love" | "Built for India" | National pride + relevance |
| "Try it free" | "14-day money-back guarantee" | Indians prefer refund guarantee over free trials |
| "Contact us" | "Call +91-XXXXXXXXXX" | Phone-first culture in India |
| "Learn more" | "See how it works" | Action-oriented, not passive |
| "Trusted by 10,000+" | "Trusted by founders across Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi, Pune" | City-specific trust signals |

### 5.2 Trust-Building Elements for Indian Market

Include these elements in all copy:

1. **Phone number prominently displayed** — Indian businesses trust phone-first communication
2. **GST number visible** — Shows legitimacy and compliance
3. **Indian pricing in INR** — No USD-only pricing
4. **City-specific references** — "Built in India, for India"
5. **WhatsApp CTA** — "Chat with us on WhatsApp" alongside email
6. **Specific numbers over ranges** — "₹4,999" not "Starting at ₹X"
7. **Money-back guarantee** — "14-day money-back. No questions asked."
8. **Real names and cities in testimonials** — "Arjun, Indie Hacker, Bangalore"
9. **Specific outcomes** — "Digitized a clinic in 2 days — charged ₹45,000"
10. **Indian payment methods** — "Pay via UPI, cards, or net banking"

### 5.3 Tone Guidelines

| Attribute | Guideline | Example |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Authority | Confident, not arrogant | "Built for India's 60M MSMEs" |
| Urgency | Specific deadlines, not fake scarcity | "Launching Monday at 10 AM IST" |
| Technicality | Explain simply, don't dumb down | "Razorpay integration that works" not "Payment abstraction layer" |
| Emotion | Pride in building for India, not guilt | "India's first" not "Stop using Western tools" |
| Specificity | Numbers over adjectives | "22 hours saved" not "Huge time savings" |
| Action | Direct commands, not suggestions | "Get Started" not "Consider getting started" |
